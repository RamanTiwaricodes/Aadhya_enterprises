Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar, net metering, subsidies, and maintenance.

Rooftop solar consists of photovoltaic (PV) modules mounted on your building's roof that convert natural sunlight into direct current (DC) electricity. A solar inverter converts this into alternating current (AC) power, which directly powers your lights, fans, air conditioners, and other appliances, reducing your grid consumption.

As a rule of thumb, for every 100–120 units (kWh) of monthly electricity consumption, you need approximately 1 kW of solar capacity. For example, if your average monthly electricity bill is around ₹4,500 (~600 units), a 4 to 5 kW rooftop system is usually ideal.

A bi-directional net meter records both the energy imported from the electricity board and the surplus solar energy exported back to the grid. At the end of the billing cycle, you are billed only for the 'net' power consumed. Any excess export is credited toward subsequent months.

Yes, solar is suitable for RCC flat roofs, tin-sheds, and sloped tile roofs that receive at least 4 to 5 hours of unshaded sunlight daily. You need approximately 90 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area per 1 kW of solar capacity.

The physical rooftop installation of structures, panels, and inverters typically takes 2 to 4 days for standard residential systems (3 kW to 10 kW). DISCOM net meter inspection, testing, and replacement generally take an additional 2 to 4 weeks depending on department scheduling.

Solar panels have no moving parts and require minimal maintenance. Rinsing the panels with plain water every 10 to 15 days to remove airborne dust and bird droppings ensures optimal energy production. Aadya Enterprises also offers periodic health inspections.

Under the central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and Uttar Pradesh state subsidy programs, residential consumers can receive substantial direct benefit transfers into their bank accounts. Actual subsidy depends on national guidelines, DISCOM approval, and sanctioned capacity. We help complete the portal formalities for all eligible clients.
